We exist to protect childhood across Texas. That means helping children and families find safety, justice, and healing after crimes against children while strengthening the systems that protect and support them. CACTX has always been courageous in stepping up and taking on daunting challenges when they believe it’s what is best for the children of Texas. CAC Executive Director Leadership that protects childhood For more than 30 years, CACTX has helped shape Texas’ response to crimes against children. Our leaders drive progress, strengthen partnerships, and advance solutions that help children and families find safety, justice, and healing.
