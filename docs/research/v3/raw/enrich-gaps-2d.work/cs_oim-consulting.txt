Accountant Carmelle Venter was on the front lines, managing the data for invoicing and payroll. She knew better than anyone how much was riding on them finding the right tool. She says, “We work as our own entity and also deliver professional services to our sister companies. It’s critical that we keep everything organized so we can invoice correctly and pay our people at the end of the day.” “ClickTime’s support team just went over and above. It was a privilege walking that journey with them as they built the integration with Xero. They completely eliminated our wasted processing time and allowed us to focus on more valuable tasks.” Carmelle Venter Accountant, OIM Consulting On top of accuracy and complex allocation, OIM Consulting needed a solution that could sync with their existing software. They were already using Xero for invoicing and BambooHR for HR management. Since neither system could capture time utilization, the firm had been using Excel spreadsheets to track hours and entering the data manually into both systems. Venter says, “It was time-consuming, that’s all I can say. There was a lot of duplicating data, just copying it from one form to another.” The process left a lot of room for human error, which compounded the administrative burden. Before Venter could even begin the data entry, she and a colleague had to review consultants’ timesheets and expense submissions, track down missing info, get approvals from various teams, and make sure no one had tampered with the formulas. Between them, it took five days of work each month, and left no time for other important tasks as the company grew. Knowing that timekeeping automation would relieve these frustrating inefficiencies, OIM Consulting began the search for a tool that could: Sync with BambooHR to automate HR information Integrate with Xero to streamline invoicing and information required for payroll Eliminate human error in timekeeping data Free up time for more value-building activities After some experimenting, they discovered ClickTime’s existing integration with BambooHR and learned about the possibility of building a custom integration. A few meetings later with ClickTime’s talented support team, they began the exciting journey of partnering with them to pioneer the integration with Xero. Venter says, “Time is money. The less time that you are spending capturing data, the more time you can spend analyzing it and building value in your business.” “Time is money. As impressed as she was with the HR improvements, Venter knew that the real wow factor would come when they could also sync the error-free timekeeping with Xero. The connection eliminated a large part of Venter’s data entry, along with potential errors in staff compensation. “ClickTime solved our problem with missing information. We configured a setting to ensure that consultants account for a minimum of 40 hours per week or eight hours per day. Now, our time is never short-captured,” she explained. She said, “The integration with Xero was going to eliminate even more labor from our administration process and make our invoicing simpler and more accurate.” Venter was blown away by how easy it was to prepare for the new integration. Venter says, “They made the journey so simple for us, communicating and addressing our needs in a way that was easy to understand. With their help, the programming part on our end didn’t seem scary at all.” She says that ClickTime support was always only an email away, and knowing they had things under control took all the stress out of the experience. The efficiencies of the dual integrations brought intense satisfaction, freeing Venter and her team to contribute more to the firm’s growth. The integrations are always relevant, up-to-date and accurate.” Although the summarized data was technically available in the old Excel-spreadsheet system, Venter is keenly aware of how much effort it would take to build the reporting herself. “Being able to see the allocations of our consultants on the dashboard is a huge benefit.
