 The Challenges Cascade Faced
 Managing a hybrid workforce of 800 employees posed several 
 challenges for Cascade. Without a centralized system, ensuring productivity, 
 accountability, and transparency of work became difficult. As a result, Cascade 
 struggled with inefficiencies, a lack of real-time monitoring, and the need for better 
 workforce management. Here are the key issues they faced: 
 Limited Work Visibility Struggled to monitor employee 
 engagement, task progress, and overall productivity in a remote setup. 
 Productivity Concerns Ensuring focus, efficiency, and 
 consistent output across a hybrid workforce was challenging. 
 Active Time Tracking Issues No real-time insights into 
 work activity, making it difficult to differentiate between productive work and idle 
 time. 
 Inaccurate Attendance & 
 Time Tracking Manual logging led to inconsistencies, inefficiencies, and unreliable 
 work-hour records. 
 The Solution Time Champ Offered
 To address these challenges, Cascade integrated Time Champ, a powerful 
 employee monitoring and workforce management solution. The implementation led to significant 
 improvements:
 Fraud Detection Identified and flagged suspicious 
 activities, preventing time-tracking manipulation and ensuring accurate work 
 hours. 
 Real-Time Work Insights Provided instant visibility into 
 work hours, active vs. idle time, and engagement, helping optimize workforce 
 efficiency. 
 Website & App Usage Tracking Analyzed which tools and 
 applications employees used, ensuring focus on high-priority tasks. 
 Automated Screenshots & Activity Logs Captured random 
 screenshots, offering verifiable proof of work progress while reducing manual 
 oversight. 
 By leveraging Time Champ’s advanced monitoring features, Cascade 
 eliminated inefficiencies, strengthened accountability, and improved remote workforce 
 management.

 What Cascade Likes Most About Time Champ
 One of the standouts features that Cascade valued most about Time 
 Champ was its ability to bring transparency and control to team management. Unlike 
 conventional tracking tools, Time Champ provided a clear, real-time view of employee work 
 habits, ensuring that productivity wasn’t just assumed but accurately measured. 
 The suspicious activity alerts were particularly useful in identifying 
 unusual behavior, such as employees using mouse jiggers or other tricks to appear active. 
 This feature helped Cascade maintain fairness and accountability, ensuring that work hours 
 reflected actual effort. 
 Additionally, Time Champ’s detailed reports on app and website usage gave Cascade 
 better insight into how employees spent their time. With customizable settings and automated timesheets, 
 managers could track productivity without micromanaging, leading to more efficient workflows and improved 
 team performance.
