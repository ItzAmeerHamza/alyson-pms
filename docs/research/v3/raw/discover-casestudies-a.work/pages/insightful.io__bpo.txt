 FAQ
 What enterprise leaders ask us most
 What is BPO software, and what does Insightful cover? 
 BPO software reports the hours, coverage and evidence behind outsourced delivery, account by account. Insightful is a work insights platform. It collects hours, application use and meeting load from desk-based teams and reports them by team, each measure against the period before. For a BPO that means each client's hours, the approver on any manual edit, and coverage against every account's queue. Call quality sampling and handle-time QA are covered on the Call Center page.
 Can clients access their own performance data? 
 Yes, scoped to their own account. A client login shows that client's hours and nothing else, so a questioned line is answered from their own view rather than from a spreadsheet somebody rebuilt the night before. What they see is the hours, the approver on any manual edit, and the activity behind them. What they never see is another client's account.
 How does Insightful keep each client's data isolated? 
 Each client's view is scoped to their own account's hours. Deployment can run on-premise or through your own directory where a contract requires it. Collection scope is set per client team before deployment rather than after. With the Audit Logs add-on, every admin change in the workspace is held in a record no user can alter, which is what a client's security questionnaire asks for.
 Does Insightful work across remote, hybrid and distributed teams? 
 Yes. It runs on the Insightful desktop application on Windows, macOS and Linux, deployed from your MDM or through Active Directory with no seat minimum, so agents at home, in a delivery center or split across sites all report the same way, by team, including operations across the US, LATAM, the Philippines and EMEA. Work setting shows as office or remote rather than as anyone's location. A field or facilities workforce without a primary computer is not covered, which is worth settling before an account is scoped.
 What compliance certifications does Insightful hold? 
 Certified: SOC 2 Type II. Compliant: HIPAA, GDPR and CCPA, which are regulations rather than certifications. The wording is deliberate, because a client's reviewer will check it. Underneath that, AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2 in transit, keys in Google Cloud KMS, and residency in the US, the EU or Saudi Arabia, or on-premise. Documentation is available under NDA.
