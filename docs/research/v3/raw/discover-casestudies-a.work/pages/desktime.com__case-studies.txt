 How much can DeskTime improve team productivity?
 DeskTime helps companies uncover productivity patterns across their teams. For example, DGI reported that using DeskTime allowed them to identify inefficient workflows and optimize tasks, which consistently increased productivity. 
 By tracking app usage, project time, and offline work, teams gain actionable insights that can lift overall performance and help employees focus on high-value activities.
 Can DeskTime save time on administrative tasks like payroll?
 Yes. Montway Auto Transport and Hire Live Support both saw dramatic reductions in manual payroll preparation. DeskTime automatically tracks work hours, eliminating paper timesheets and manual entry. 
 This freed HR teams to focus on higher-value work while ensuring accurate, reliable data for salaries, bonuses, and appraisals, saving hours every week and reducing potential errors.
 Is DeskTime easy to implement for new teams?
 Absolutely. DeskTime’s onboarding process is quick and user-friendly, requiring minimal setup. Antino Labs and On The Map Marketing noted that their teams started using DeskTime within days, not weeks. 
 Employees can track time automatically, log offline work, and personalize private time, making adoption smooth even for global or remote teams.
 How does DeskTime ensure accurate and transparent data?
 DeskTime records app usage, project time, and offline activity with 99% accuracy compared to manual tracking. 
 TrueSix and AD VERBUM reported that this data provides transparency, both internally and for clients, allowing managers to fairly evaluate contributions and make informed decisions. Accurate logs also help identify bottlenecks and optimize workflows without micromanagement.
 Does DeskTime respect employee privacy while improving performance?
 Yes. DeskTime is GDPR and ISO 27001 compliant, offering features such as Private Time and Offline Time , which give employees control over their personal or non-work activities. 
 Setupad and TrueSix highlighted that their teams feel trusted rather than surveilled, while managers still gain insights into productivity. This balance boosts accountability and engagement without compromising privacy.
