 How Hubstaff’s productivity monitoring works
 Track time with Hubstaff apps 
 Have employees, contractors, and other team members install the Hubstaff app on desktop, mobile, or Chrome and begin tracking time to tasks and projects. Every productivity metric is built on accurate time tracking to ensure accuracy and efficiency. 
 Capture productivity signals 
 While the timer runs, Hubstaff records key productivity data on desktop, such as app & URL usage , activity levels, idle time, and optional screenshots. Each feature can be enabled or disabled to fit your workflow, and screenshots can be blurred or turned off entirely. Hubstaff records context, never content.
 Review insights and reports 
 Hubstaff turns raw productivity signals into real-time productivity dashboards and 20+ reports . Managers can customize their dashboard to see live updates on productivity drops, workloads, and focus patterns using drag-and-drop widgets.
 Data can then be filtered by employee, project, or timeframe, and exported for clients, key stakeholders, or internal reviews. Reports can be shared with specific people via role-based permissions or exported for compliance and audit purposes.
 Key features of Hubstaff’s productivity monitoring 
 Hubstaff provides a clear, low-intrusion view of how work time is used. Instead of surveillance, it focuses on tangible data, context, and productivity signals that help teams stay focused and work more effectively. 
 Activity levels 
 Hubstaff does not perform keystroke logging under any circumstances. Instead, keyboard and mouse activity percentages serve as a simple signal of engagement, not a definitive measure of productivity.
 Low activity during training sessions, meetings, or calls is normal — it’s the context that matters.
 App & URL tracking 
 See which apps and websites are used during tracked time for smoother workflows and optimized tech budgets. A dedicated AI tools category highlights the usage of tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, with customizable rules based on team or role.
 Or, try the Insights add-on for productive/unproductive classifications to help reduce wasted hours.
 Idle time tracking 
 Detect and manage idle time for more accurate time tracking, timesheets, and payments. Alerts help you spot idle timeouts in real time to prevent time theft, burnout, and other issues before it’s too late.
 Achievement badges 
 Turn productivity tracking into motivation, not micromanagement. Reward consistent focus with Achievements that recognize employee efficiency, completed tasks, and time goals. You’ll also gain better insight into unhealthy work patterns or overwork. 
 Optional screenshots 
 Capture visual context only when needed with optional, fully configurable screenshots. You can customize time tracking with screenshots , change frequency, and enable blurring.
 Apply different settings by role , department, or team to support internal governance and regional compliance requirements. Employees also have visibility into their own monitoring data, aligning with Hubstaff's guiding principles of transparency and trust.
 Workforce analytics 
 Go beyond day-to-day productivity monitoring with advanced workforce analytics that highlight unusual activity , workload imbalances, department-level productivity trends, and optimization opportunities.
 Balance utilization rates, plan capacity more effectively, and reduce unnecessary meetings. Benchmark productivity across teams to identify long-term trends, compare remote and in-office performance , and spot opportunities to improve compliance with structured, data-driven reporting.
 Access controls for larger teams 
 Deploy productivity monitoring confidently with security and governance features made to support the needs of larger teams operating in the regulated sector.
 Hubstaff supports Single Sign-On ( SSO ), role-based permissions, configurable data retention, and audit-ready reporting to help IT, HR, and compliance teams manage workforce data securely with system-defined data retention windows built for regulated environments.

 How productivity monitoring differs from employee monitoring 
 Employee monitoring focuses on accountability and compliance, capturing things like which apps and URLs are used, screenshots, and evidence of activity. Productivity monitoring, on the other hand, centers on efficiency and trends.
 It’s about helping teams identify productivity trends, reduce burnout, and improve efficiency without micromanagement. Many organizations need both: employee monitoring to meet compliance requirements and productivity monitoring to improve team performance. Hubstaff brings these capabilities together, allowing large organizations the ability to monitor without micromanaging .
 Hubstaff’s guiding principles
 Principle 1: Transparency
 Hubstaff is designed to provide clarity, not an excuse to micromanage. Users are in control of when they track time, take breaks, and submit timesheets, and they can remove sensitive data as needed. The software also ensures monitoring is connected only to active work time on employee-owned devices.
 Principle 2: Access
 Both employees and managers have access to performance data like app and URL usage, keyboard and mouse activity levels, and more. Hubstaff only tracks time on personal devices when users are clocked in, and data is never sold to third parties. 
 Principle 3: Control
 Hubstaff enables users to tailor the software to their managerial style and compliance needs, with data access available only to specific roles, the option to customize screenshots, and visibility into app and URL usage and activity levels that matter. Hubstaff avoids invasive practices like keystroke logging, webcam recording, and email monitoring. Hubstaff also supports regulated industries with SOC 2 Type II certification, GDPR compliance, and HIPAA -ready deployments, giving teams flexibility and options to meet regulatory requirements.

 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
 Your questions, answered
 Can I use Hubstaff to analyze productivity trends over time?
 Yes. Hubstaff can analyze productivity trends with a fully customizable dashboard that provides live updates on employee activity levels, website and app usage, earnings, PTO, and more. There are also over 20+ detailed reports for time , activity, expenses, and more. 
 Is employee privacy protected when using productivity monitoring tools?
 Tools protect employee privacy when they prioritize user control , data protection, and adhere to compliance standards like GDPR , HIPAA , and SOC 2 . Tools like Hubstaff offer customizable permissions and enterprise-grade compliance, and avoid intrusive practices like keystroke logging and webcam shots . 
 What types of teams benefit from productivity monitoring?
 Productivity monitoring benefits virtually any team. Whether you work remotely, hybrid, in-office, or in the field, productivity monitoring tools can help organizations of all sizes reach new heights. They’re especially useful for BPOs, VAs, agencies, construction, healthcare, and various other industries.
 Can productivity monitoring help reduce burnout?
 Yes. While productivity monitoring is also associated with invasive tactics and potential for micromanagement , it can also be used to give managers the ability to check in on the mental health of remote, hybrid, or distributed team employees they might not have face-to-face interactions with.
 What should I look for when choosing productivity monitoring software?
 It’s important to look for a productivity monitoring tool that fits both your budget and your ethics surrounding productivity monitoring . Test drive tools that allow you to customize monitoring features to fit your unique management style and compliance needs.
 Does Hubstaff integrate with project management or payroll tools?
 Yes. Hubstaff integrates with over 35 tools for project management, accounting, payroll, and CRM like Asana, QuickBooks, PayPal, and Salesforce. 
 Is productivity monitoring software expensive?
 The cost of productivity monitoring software depends on the company and the extent of your monitoring needs. Most offer flexible pricing plans that scale with your team's growth, providing you with the features you need while you set the pace. 
 Is Hubstaff’s productivity monitoring software compliant with HIPAA, GDPR, and SOC 2?
 Yes, Hubstaff’s productivity monitoring software can be configured to comply with HIPAA, GDPR, and SOC2 requirements. Hubstaff ensures organizations have the support for compliance requirements they need through configurable monitoring settings, role-based permissions, audit-ready reporting, and security controls. Companies can configure the platform to align with internal policies and regulatory requirements, including HIPAA, GDPR, and SOC 2.
