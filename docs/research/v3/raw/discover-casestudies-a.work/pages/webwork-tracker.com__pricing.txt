 FAQs
 Does the free trial include all features? 
 Yes. During the free trial, you can try all pricing plans and use all the features that way.
 Can I change my plan anytime? 
 Yes, you can change your plan anytime. You can either upgrade to a higher plan or downgrade to a lower plan. Note that changing a plan will affect your whole workspace.
 What types of payment do you accept? 
 We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and PayPal.
 Do I pay for my own seat as the workspace owner? 
 Yes. The workspace owner is a member with full access and is counted like every other member.
 Should I pay for Project Viewers as well? 
 No, Project Viewers are not counted as members so you will not have to pay for them.
 How does the yearly subscription work?
