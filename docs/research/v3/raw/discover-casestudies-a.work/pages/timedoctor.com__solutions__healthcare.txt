 How does Time Doctor help prevent burnout in healthcare teams? 
 Time Doctor highlights early signs of burnout by showing extended shifts, irregular handoffs, and missed meal or rest breaks, which are widespread in clinical and support roles. Nurse managers, schedulers, and team leads can use this data to rebalance shifts, ensure fair workload distribution, and maintain consistent staffing without overburdening care teams.
 Can Time Doctor integrate with our existing EHR or billing systems? 
 Yes. Time Doctor integrates with tools like QuickBooks, ADP, and Paychex, and works seamlessly alongside EHR, patient scheduling, and billing systems. 
 This helps healthcare teams stay focused on care delivery by reducing manual data entry, avoiding system switching, and maintaining consistent workflows across departments.
 How does Time Doctor support HIPAA and GDPR compliance? 
 Time Doctor features include privacy-focused tools such as role-based access controls, audit-ready activity logs, and data visibility settings.  
 These tools enable healthcare teams to meet HIPAA, GDPR, and SOC 2 requirements while protecting patient data and promoting internal accountability. 
 How can Time Doctor help manage field-based and mobile healthcare teams?
