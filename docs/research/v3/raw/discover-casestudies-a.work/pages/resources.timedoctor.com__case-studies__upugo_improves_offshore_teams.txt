 upUgo improves offshore team management and task tracking with Time Doctor
 At a glance
 upUgo is a digital marketing agency that helps make brands better, headquartered in Bristol, UK, and led by CEO Luke Sartain.
 upUgo offers technical SEO, digital PR, influencer marketing, content creation, social media, and video production services to provide companies with best in class omnichannel marketing campaigns.
 Founded
 2014
 Company Size
 101 – 500
 Industry
 Marketing and Advertising
 Location
 UK
 Work Type
 Remote
 The challenges
 upUgo, like many other Time Doctor users, has part of their team working offshore, away from the main headquarters in the UK.
 Managing offshore teams can present many challenges but upUgo was specifically struggling with being able to quantify the work that was being completed by their offshore team.
 The solutions
 upUgo began searching for a time tracking tool that would help create more transparency and visibility for its distributed teams.
 Time Doctor was the perfect solution because it not only offered the time tracking solution that they were looking for but it also provided insights into the quality of work being produced.
 Luke shared that with Time Doctor, upUgo gained valuable company-wide insights into the time required to complete certain tasks.
 I can’t recommend it enough. It’s one of the main secrets to scaling successfully and I’m more than happy to recommend it to anyone else.
 Luke Sartain
 upUgo
 The results
 The efficiency of upUgos management of offshore teams has improved by being able to see what is being worked on in real-time and by tracking how long each task takes to complete. Time Doctor s workday insights have enabled upUgo to make data-driven decisions that help the company scale successfully and maintain its quality standards.
 To hear more from Luke on why upUgo loves Time Doctor, watch this quick video.
 Tracking
 By tracking all activity in real-time, managers can ensure that work is being completed on time by offshore teams.
 Integrations
 With 60+ integration options, organizations gain more insights into what is being worked on in other applications from one centralized platform.
 Dashboard
 Dashboards and timeline breakdowns create visibility into how long tasks are taking, allowing managers and employees to manage deadlines and stay on track.
 Screenshots & activity levels
 Through detailed analytic reports and screenshots, managers are able to quickly perform quality assurance checks for offshore teams.
