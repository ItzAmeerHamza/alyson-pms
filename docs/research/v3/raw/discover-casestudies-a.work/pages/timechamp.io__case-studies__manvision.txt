 The Challenges ManVision Faced 
 ManVision works with teams spread across different locations, and that setup brought 
 some real challenges. The 
 company did not have an automated system to monitor the attendance, breaks, and idle 
 time of employees, which 
 created inefficiencies in the monitoring of employees and optimization of their output. 
 Such constraints impeded 
 proper reporting, adherence, and performance. In general, it suppressed the performance 
 of the company. 
 Here are the key obstacles ManVision faced: 
 Lack of Attendance Visibility – 
 Unable to track check-ins, check-outs, and daily availability, affecting planning. 
 Unmonitored Idle Time – 
 No applications to track periods of inactivity, so the loss of productivity goes 
 unnoticed. 
 Inconsistent Break Tracking – 
 Breaks weren’t recorded, making it hard to enforce policies or optimize routines. 
 Limited Productivity Insights – 
 No real-time data to separate active work from downtime, hindering analysis. 
 The Solution Time Champ Offered 
 ManVision was looking for a simple solution to improve employee accountability, cut down on

 getting better quickly.

 Automated Attendance & Break Tracking – 
 Time Champ made it easy to automatically log employee attendance and breaks, reduced the 
 need for manual tracking, and ensured consistency across the team. 
 Comprehensive Attendance Reports –

 logouts, which helped enforce discipline and punctuality. 
 Idle Tracking & Optimization – 
 Time Champ offered insights into idle time and intervals, enabling ManVision to reduce 
 downtime and improve efficiency. 
 Boost in Productivity –
