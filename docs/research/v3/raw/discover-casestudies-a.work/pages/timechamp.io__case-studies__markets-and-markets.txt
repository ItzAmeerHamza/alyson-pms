 Beyond this, they highlighted additional challenges that were impacting their workforce efficiency:
 Lack of real-time visibility into employee work hours and productivity trends. 
 Inconsistent tracking of project-based work, leading to inefficiencies in resource allocation. 
 The need for comprehensive reporting to assess individual and team performance. 
 Ensuring employees maintained a healthy work-life balance without compromising output. 
 Difficulty in identifying productivity bottlenecks and implementing corrective measures. 
 Markets and Markets required an advanced workforce management solution that would provide a clear, data-driven overview of employee activities, facilitate better decision-making, and improve overall operational efficiency.
