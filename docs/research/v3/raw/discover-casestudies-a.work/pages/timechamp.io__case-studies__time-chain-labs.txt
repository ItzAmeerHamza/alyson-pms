 The Challenges Timechain Labs Faced 
 Timechain Labs, a small yet fully remote team, had to deal with its own set of difficulties. As the employees 
 simultaneously juggled various tasks, the company had a hard time maintaining consistent productivity, commitment, 
 and task ownership. It also did not have a centralized system that further restricted visibility and control of 
 remote operations. 
 Here are the key issues they faced: 
 Low Work Visibility – 
 There were no clear details of how team members were allocating their time or their work progress. 
 Missed Deadlines – 
 Failed to meet project deadlines and did not spend enough time on the assigned tasks because of distractions. 
 No Performance Monitoring – 
 No real-time tracking or valid data in the reviews and feedback. 
 Lack of Accountability – 
 Without systematic tracking, team members could not be held accountable for deliverables and work hours. 
 The Solution Time Champ Offered 
 Timechain Labs has adopted the Time Champ as a comprehensive solution for remote workforce monitoring to effectively 
 address its time management and team responsibility issues. The platform brought much-needed clarity and consistency 
 to daily processes, enabling employers to monitor productivity and review performance effectively. 
 Here’s how Time Champ delivered results: 
 Screenshot Monitoring – 
 Automatic screenshots provided a visual record of work in a day and enabled managers to maintain accountability without necessarily having to supervise employees. 
 Live Screen Monitoring – 
 It allowed HR and team leads to monitor real-time activities, which gives immediate visibility of what is being done. 
 Automated Attendance Tracking – 
 Automated check-in and check-out systems, accurate attendance records for monthly reviews. 
 Performance-Based Insights – 
 Tracking of apps and websites usage with comprehensive activity reports to produce reliable, data-driven reports on performance review and feedback. 
 Through these tools, Timechain Labs managed to implement greater clarity, fair tracking, and make performance management easier across its remote team.
