 Download the Hubstaff employee monitoring app 
 Users can install Hubstaff’s desktop, mobile, or web apps for Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS, or Android. Monitoring features are optional and fully customizable for desktop users, while the mobile app offers time tracking and GPS location tracking for distributed teams.

 Hubstaff’s guiding principles
 Principle 1: Transparency
 Monitoring features are built to add visibility into employee performance for both individual contributors and their managers. Use this performance data to inform staffing, compliance, or client reporting decisions while users gain transparency into what’s being tracked and how the data will be used.
 Principle 2: Access
 Hubstaff’s configurable role-based permissions let IT and compliance teams configure screenshots, app and URL usage, and activity levels to standardize policies across roles, teams, and regions. Hubstaff does not rely on keystroke logging, email monitoring, or camera access.
 Principle 3: Control
 Users decide when to track time, take breaks, and submit hours, keeping monitoring intentional and tied to time tracking. They also have the option to delete sensitive data as needed. Tracking of all forms stops immediately once the timer is turned off.

 How Hubstaff supports secure and transparent employee monitoring
 Storing screenshots
 Optional screenshots are stored encrypted and transmitted via SSL over HTTPS. Employees can open and review their own screenshots at any time, so there's never a question about what's been captured.
 Lets employees be in full control of their data
 Employees decide when the timer starts and stops, and their tracked data belongs to them. They can delete time entries and any screenshots that contain personal information whenever they need to.
 Monitoring during work hours only
 Hubstaff tracks time and activity only while the timer is running, collecting no data outside of active work sessions on personal devices. On company-owned devices, tracking is admin-configured and employees are informed of the monitoring policy in place.

 Your questions, answered
 Frequently asked questions
 What is employee monitoring software?
 Employee monitoring software gives managers visibility into how work happens by tracking work hours, app and URL usage, activity levels, and optional screenshots. Teams use it to understand productivity, support compliance, and share proof of work with clients.
 What features should I look for in workplace monitoring software?
 Look for time tracking, app and URL reporting, activity measurement, optional and configurable screenshots, role-based permissions, and detailed reporting. Privacy controls matter just as much, so choose a tool that lets you match tracking to your policies and regional requirements.
 What are examples of employee monitoring?
 Common examples include time tracking, app and website usage reports, keyboard and mouse activity levels, optional screenshots, and GPS tracking for field teams. More invasive methods like keystroke logging, webcam access, and email monitoring also exist, and Hubstaff doesn't use them.
 How do employees feel about being monitored?
 It depends heavily on how monitoring is introduced and how the data is used. Clear policies, employee access to their own data, configurable tracking, and managers who read activity in context all reduce distrust and disengagement. Monitoring on its own doesn't improve morale or productivity; transparency around it is what builds acceptance.
 Does Hubstaff log keystrokes or monitor emails?
 No. Hubstaff doesn't use keystroke logging, email monitoring, or camera access. It measures keyboard and mouse activity levels without capturing what you type, so teams get visibility without invasive data collection.
 Does Hubstaff work for work-from-home and hybrid teams?
 Yes. Hubstaff runs on personal devices, company-issued devices, and mobile, so it fits distributed, hybrid, and in-office setups. Employees track time with the desktop app on their own devices, while company-owned devices support admin-configured tracking.
 How does Hubstaff support GDPR and HIPAA compliance?
 Hubstaff meets SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR, LGPD, and CCPA standards, with configurable permissions and data controls that help your team apply its own policies. Visit the Hubstaff Trust Center for more details.
