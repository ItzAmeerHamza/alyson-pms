 The Obstacles Infosys Faced
 Compliance with Overtime Laws: Japan has very clear laws about 
 overtime. The company needed a system that could keep a close eye on working hours to ensure employees 
 were not pushed beyond the legal limits. The goal was to avoid burnout and ensure every employee 
 enjoyed a healthy work-life balance, as required by local laws. 
 Accurate Client Billing: With multiple projects and a high standard 
 of client service, ensuring that every billing hour was correctly recorded was critical. Manual 
 processes were prone to error, and any mistake could affect client trust and lead to financial 
 discrepancies. 
 Employee Transparency & Trust: Beyond simply tracking time, Infosys 
 Japan wanted to ensure that its workforce felt respected. They needed a tool that not only recorded 
 work hours but also allowed employees to share feedback about the process. By gathering input and 
 securing consent for tracking, the company aimed to build trust and improve the overall employee 
 experience. 
 These challenges were not just about numbers and rules, they were about creating a 
 supportive and transparent work environment. The division needed a solution that was clear, easy to use, and 
 aligned with the company’s core values of care and excellence.
 The Solution Time Champ Offered 
 The team found Time Champ to be a practical and empowering solution. Time Champ was 
 designed to do much more than simply track time, it was built to improve how work gets done. Here’s how Time 
 Champ addressed each challenge:
 Smart Automated Tracking: Time Champ replaced manual entries with a 
 smart, automated system. Every second of work was logged accurately, reducing errors and ensuring that 
 billing hours were always correct. 
 Real-Time Alerts: Time Champ featured instant alerts when employees began 
 to approach overtime limits. Managers could see these alerts and take action immediately, ensuring that no 
 one was overworked. 
 Productivity and Balance Insights: Time Champ didn’t stop at tracking 
 time. It also provided clear insights into productivity and work-life balance, helping managers adjust 
 workloads without compromising well-being. 
 Transparent Employee Feedback: Understanding that trust is built on 
 transparency, Time Champ implemented a feedback module. Employees shared feedback effortlessly about the 
 tool, giving the management team valuable insights. Every update made reflected employee input, keeping 
 the system ethical and user-friendly.

 meet its
