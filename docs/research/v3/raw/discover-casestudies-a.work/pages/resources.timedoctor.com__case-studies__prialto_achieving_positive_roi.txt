 Achieving positive ROI in one month with Time Doctor
 At a glance
 For a busy executive, time is their biggest asset. Most executives have too much to do and not enough time to do it. Prialto helps busy executives scale their admin needs with a managed virtual assistant service.
 Founded
 2009
 Company Size
 101 – 500
 Industry
 Outsourcing/Offshoring
 Location
 US
 Work Type
 Hybrid
 The challenges
 Prialto solves the problem of trying to find a single assistant with a superhuman skill set. Instead of requiring one person to meet every need, executives that join Prialto can access a team of assistants who are trained to complete a large variety of tasks including building an executive’s network, making meetings and following up with sales leads. 
 Prialto wanted to implement a time track.
 The solutions
 By using Time Doctor, Prialto has been able to see how its assistants spend their time, leading to more targeted training on ways to streamline workflows. Additionally, the company was able to spot areas where tools could be developed to automate tasks and achieve positive ROI in a month.
 Time Doctor’s analytics and features like Screencasts have enabled Prialto to openly share productivity data with its clients and reassure them about the security of Prialto’s processes. Screen captures are used to audit and address cases where assistants are inadvertently putting clients’ security at risk.
 3 favorite Time Doctor features
 Quick view dashboard
 Prialto has found the dashboard useful for glancing at key metrics without spending an inordinate amount of time getting stuck in data minutia.
 Friendly customizable
pop-ups
 Pop-up reminders have helped keep PAs on track with their work. The fact that the frequency and type of pop-ups can be customized prevents them from becoming annoying.
 Easy breakdown reporting
 Easy to use and self-explanatory reporting features have improved decision-making across the Prialto team.
 Conclusion
 The ability to provide reports to customers has helped Prialto keep and grow many accounts, resulting in substantial growth of monthly recurring revenue.
 Coaching and workflow optimizations enabled Time Doctor have also created a better work environment and reduced employee turnover, leading to major annual cost savings.
 “I would not venture to advise another company. Each one is unique and each founder/entrepreneur is in a unique situation. But I would advise busy executives who retain a service to leverage remote help as follows: Always ask how the service manages their employees time and how you’ll know exactly what the service is doing for you on a given day. If they can’t give you an answer, the service has probably not deployed the right systems and time tracking tools to provide you with a high level of service.
 “Utilization of reports have resulted in substantial growth.” 
 With Time Doctor, we saw a positive ROI our first month.
 Eric Taussig
 Prialto
