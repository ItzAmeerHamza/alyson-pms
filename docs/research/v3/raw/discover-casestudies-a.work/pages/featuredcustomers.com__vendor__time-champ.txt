 Time Champ
 Read 22 Time Champ reviews and testimonials from customers, explore 21 case studies and customer success stories, and watch customer videos to see why companies chose Time Champ as their Leave of Absence Software
 Time Champ is a Workforce Intelligence Platform that gives operations, HR, and leadership teams complete, real-time visibility into how work happens across every team, location, and working model: remote, hybrid, field, and in-office. Employee Monitoring- Track activity, application usage, idle time, screenshots, and behaviour patterns in real time. Clear, objective data for managers who need accountability without constant surveillance. Time Tracking- Automatically log work hours, allocate time to projects and tasks, and generate verified timesheets with zero manual input. No errors. No disputes.
