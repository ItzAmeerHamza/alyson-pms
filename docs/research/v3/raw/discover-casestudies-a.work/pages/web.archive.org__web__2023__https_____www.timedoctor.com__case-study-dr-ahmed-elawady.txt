 Time Doctor helped increase productivity by 15% per employee.
 Dr. Ahmed Elawady
 Bokdoc 
 BokDoc is a specialized marketplace for surgeries located in Egypt. Their platform makes booking surgical procedures quick and stress free. Patients are able to compare surgeons, ask questions, upload medical documents and schedule exams and procedures all in one place.
 In 2020, COVID-19 forced the entire company of 17 team members to enter a remote work environment.
 Like most CEO's experiencing remote work for the first time, Dr. Ahmed Elawady was worried about the productivity of his employees while they adjust to their new work from home situation.
 On the recommendation of a consultant, BokDoc installed Time Doctor on everyone’s laptop or workstation and saw an average productivity increase of 15% per employee.
