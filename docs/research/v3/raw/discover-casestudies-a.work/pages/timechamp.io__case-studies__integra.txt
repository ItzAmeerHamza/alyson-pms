 The Challenges Integra Faced 
 Behind Integra’s legacy of excellence were a few quiet struggles that made everyday 
 operations harder than they 
 needed to be. Before Time Champ, some key gaps in visibility and accountability began to 
 hold teams back. 
 Unclear Work Patterns – 
 Many employees appeared busy, but actual active work hours often told a different story, 
 especially in remote settings, where tracking was nearly impossible. 
 Frequent Idle Time – 
 Long stretches of unproductive hours went unnoticed, affecting timelines and team 
 efficiency without any clear explanation. 
 Lack of Remote Clarity – 
 Managers had little insight into what remote teams were working on, making it hard to 
 offer support or catch red flags early. 
 Missing Activity Records – 
 Without accurate, reliable data, important details slipped through the cracks, making 
 performance reviews, planning, and decision-making more difficult than they should’ve 
 been. 
 The Solution Time Champ Offered 
 Integra needed to overcome the challenges and reintroduce structure to everyday activities, 
 so it reached out to a 
 smart and intuitive solution called Time Champ that would do more than simply tracking. 
 Personalized Dashboards – 
 Each of the employees had clear visibility about the productivity status, and they were 
 kept focused, motivated, and on track. 
 Anomaly Detection Reports – 
 Flagged unusual patterns in work behavior early, so managers could step in with support, 
 not just supervision. 
 Live Screen & Activity Monitoring – 
 Enabled real-time visibility through screenshots, mouse clicks, and keystroke tracking, 
 building trust through transparency. 
 Custom Reporting Tools – 
 Delivered insightful, built-in reports for each team, making oversight simpler and 
 smarter. 
 Deeper Accountability – 
 With detailed activity logs, remote teams could be supported more effectively, ensuring 
 no time or effort went unnoticed.
