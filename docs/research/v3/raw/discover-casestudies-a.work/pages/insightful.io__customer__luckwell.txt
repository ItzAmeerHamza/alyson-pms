 Company Overview:
 Luckwell Solutions is a California-based healthcare-focused BPO with delivery teams in the Philippines. The company supports U.S. home health agencies by providing administrative, QA, and IT services that help clients scale efficiently while maintaining operational and compliance standards.
 ‍
 Services:
 Administrative support, quality assurance services, IT support services, workforce operations for U.S. healthcare organizations, primarily home health agencies.
 Before Insightful, our evidence didn’t always carry weight. Now, when the data comes from Insightful, it speaks for itself.
 Terei Asido 
 Senior Director, IT Systems and Cybersecurity, Data Privacy Officer, Luckwell Business Solutions
 THE CHALLENGE 
 Manual Reporting and Fragmented Tools Slowed Decisions and Visibility
 ‍
 When Terei joined Luckwell Business Solutions, he needed to pilot a transformation. Leading a team responsible for remote monitoring, productivity, and attendance insights, he had to turn raw data into actionable intelligence. These insights would become the engine behind smarter resource planning, precise attendance tracking, and stronger performance management. Thanks to Terei and his team, leadership gained the confidence to make data-driven decisions, keeping operations seamless and efficient, no matter where the workforce was located.
 ‍
 But before the transformation:
‍
 Managers spent valuable hours compiling manual reports
 HR frequently questioned evidence because it lacked depth and consistency
 Decisions about staffing and performance were delayed
 Multiple monitoring tools created complexity, cost, and inconsistent oversight
 To support Luckwell’s rapid growth and healthcare compliance obligations at the same time, Terei needed a platform that could give him comprehensive workforce visibility by organizing  productivity, time keeping, and security data in one place.
‍
 “Everything was manual before Insightful. We didn’t know where to get the right reports, and it slowed down operations.” 
 ‍
 ‍
 THE SOLUTION 
 Luckwell Centralizes Productivity, Time, and Security Data with Insightful
Following a three- to four-month evaluation of competing workforce intelligence solutions, Insightful stood out on cost, support, and ease of use. Once selected, Terei led a coordinated rollout across operations and IT.
‍
 Key improvements included:
‍
 Instant access to reliable reports
 Managers no longer compile spreadsheets. HR now relies on consistent, audit-ready time and attendance data.
 Greater team focus and reduced idle time
 Clear workforce visibility across all distributed teams helped his managers identify workflow gaps early and take corrective action.
 Unified monitoring for office and remote agents
 Custom alerts flag policy deviations—such as accessing non-business URLs—strengthening cybersecurity and compliance.
 Simplified IT and HR operations
 By consolidating tools, both departments now work from the same trusted source of evidence.
 ‍
 “With Insightful, we can simply go into the portal and get the data we need—whether for operations, IT, or any other department—making it much easier to support decisions and drive improvements across the company.” 
 ‍
 ‍
 THE RESULTS 
 A Shared Source of Truth for HR, IT, Facilities and Operations 
Insightful now serves as Luckwell’s accountability infrastructure—providing complete workforce visibility that powers staffing decisions, compliance checks, and IT oversight with unified, dependable data.
 ‍
 With centralized visibility, the company can:
‍
 Deliver accurate productivity and attendance reports to clients
 Respond proactively to new client requirements
 Reinforce cybersecurity and operational consistency across accounts
Maintain productivity and quality levels even as team structures shift
 These improvements have resulted in meaningful gains across the business, including:
‍
 Significantly reduced time spent on manual reporting
 More accurate staffing and resource planning
 Elimination of redundant monitoring platforms
 Greater trust in evidence used for HR, IT, and operational decisions
 ‍
 Looking ahead, Luckwell plans to expand Insightful into new service lines, including extended IT support for clients. With Insightful as its workforce analytics backbone, Luckwell can continue scaling their staff while maintaining compliance and trusted data across all teams.
 ‍
 ‍
 THE FUTURE 
 Scaling with Confidence and Trusted Data
Building on this success, Luckwell plans to expand Insightful into new service lines, including extended IT support offerings for clients. With unified workforce analytics embedded into daily operations, the company is well positioned to continue scaling its teams while maintaining compliance, data accuracy, and cross-departmental trust.
Key Takeaways: ‍
‍
 Centralizing workforce data eliminated manual reporting and delays
 A single platform improved trust in productivity and attendance evidence
 Unified visibility strengthened compliance and cross-team collaboration
Insightful gave Luckwell a reliable workforce analytics backbone that supports growth, compliance, and confident decision-making across the business.
