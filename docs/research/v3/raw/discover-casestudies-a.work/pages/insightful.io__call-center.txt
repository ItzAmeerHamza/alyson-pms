 FAQ
 What contact center leaders ask us most
 What is call center monitoring software?
 Call center monitoring software reports what agents are doing across a shift, not only during the calls. Insightful is a work insights platform. It collects hours, application use and meeting load from desk-based teams and reports them by team, each measure against the period before. On a floor that means application activity, active and idle time and hours worked, so after-call work, tool switching and the gaps between contacts sit alongside the calls QA already samples. It records no audio, which rules it out as a replacement for your recording platform and is the reason it reaches the rest of the shift.
 How is this different from our call recording or QA platform?
 It never hears the call. A recording platform captures that; this captures the shift around it, which is after-call work, the systems an agent moves through, coverage against the queue and the time between contacts. The two run side by side, because neither one tells you what the other is looking at.
 Does Insightful record calls or listen to conversations?
 No. No audio capture, no transcript, no keystroke logging. What is recorded is application and website activity, active and idle time, hours worked, and screenshots where an admin has turned them on. Screenshots are the one setting that changes the shape of this answer, and they stay off until somebody changes it.
 Can agents see their own numbers?
 In a visible rollout, yes: each agent gets a self-service view of their own activity and productive time. That is the rollout we recommend, and on a floor with real attrition it can decide whether the program lands as coaching or lands badly. It is a configuration rather than a constraint, so a stealth deployment is supported where a customer needs one, and in that case the answer to this question is no.
 What about PCI or regulated queues?
 Set them up narrower than the rest of the floor, which is what collection scope is for. Screenshots are off unless an admin enables them, and scope is agreed per team before the desktop application is deployed, so a card-handling queue can run a configuration the outbound floor does not. Insightful is SOC 2 Type II certified and compliant with HIPAA, GDPR and CCPA. That is evidence for your assessment rather than a substitute for it, and the documentation behind it is available under NDA.
