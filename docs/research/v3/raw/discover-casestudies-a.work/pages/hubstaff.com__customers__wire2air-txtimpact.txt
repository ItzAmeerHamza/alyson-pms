 Background
 Wire2Air | TXTImpact is a New York-based SaaS company that provides mass texting and business messaging services to over 5,000 clients in industries such as retail, health care, and financial services. Their remote-first team of about 50 employees operates globally in North America, Europe, and APAC.
 With a mission to help organizations reach customers through high-impact SMS campaigns, the company integrates with tools like Zapier, MailChimp, and Salesforce to deliver seamless experiences. 
 The challenge
 With their remote team spread across time zones, Wire2Air | TXTImpact faced growing pains in managing productivity, ensuring visibility, and maintaining fair workloads. 
 “Time zones, unclear workloads, and manual processes were slowing us down and straining our team.”
 As they scaled, they needed a way to coordinate their distributed team and maintain high-quality service while adapting to regulatory and market shifts.
 Their key growing pains included:
 Inconsistent time tracking across time zones
 Limited visibility into project progress and team activity
 Communication lags due to asynchronous schedules
 Unbalanced workloads that led to burnout or underperformance
 Time-consuming admin tasks like billing and reporting
 These issues not only impacted team morale but also created risks for client delivery and profitability.
 The solution
 The team turned to Hubstaff to gain operational clarity without compromising autonomy. 
 Their goals were:
 Real-time visibility into team performance
 Automated, accurate time tracking
 Clear accountability without micromanagement
 Better client billing accuracy
 Scalable workflows and compliance
 After internal evaluations and stakeholder input, Wire2Air implemented Hubstaff in phases, starting with a pilot and scaling up after building trust and refining policies.
 Why Hubstaff?
 Mamta Arun, the Director of Operations at TXTImpact, said they chose Hubstaff for its affordability, reliability, and easy remote monitoring capabilities, which make managing remote teams easier.
 Key Hubstaff features that made a difference
 Automated time tracking : Eliminated manual timesheets and standardized tracking across continents
 Activity monitoring : Offered insight into productivity trends while respecting team privacy
 Project & task integration : Synced seamlessly with their project tools for streamlined workflows
 Reporting & analytics : Delivered real-time insights for better decisions, resourcing, and billing
 How Hubstaff helped build trust, speed, and team autonomy
 In just two years, the switch to Hubstaff had a measurable impact:
 “ Hubstaff didn’t just streamline our operations, it reshaped how our teams collaborate, track time, and deliver results. It gave us transparency, trust, and the freedom to focus on what matters most: growth. ”
 25% increase in productivity
 Improved task focus and transparency helped boost project delivery speed and alignment across teams.
 50% reduction in administrative overhead
 Automated timesheets and reporting freed managers from manual tracking, saving hours weekly.
 Improved cross-team collaboration by 30%
 Role-specific dashboards and time data gave clarity on ownership and dependencies, enhancing coordination by 30%.
 Greater client trust
 Hubstaff’s clear billing and work logs improved transparency and helped avoid disputes.
 Better employee experience
 Team members felt more empowered and less micromanaged. The platform’s flexibility and autonomy-first design supported a healthier, more motivated workforce.
 Why it worked
 “Hubstaff helped us build a culture of trust and accountability powered by data, not micromanagement.”
 Hubstaff provided more than software; it helped Wire2Air shape a culture of trust, transparency, and data-driven decision-making. Privacy settings and open communication ensured team buy-in , while advanced analytics enabled managers to support growth and avoid burnout.
 What’s next
 Wire2Air | TXTImpact is continuing to scale its global presence and aims to deepen its use of Hubstaff by leveraging advanced analytics for long-term workforce planning and business development. 
 “ We see Hubstaff as a long-term enabler of operational excellence and employee well-being. ”
 The company also plans to integrate Hubstaff more fully into its broader tech stack, creating a unified operational ecosystem. Team feedback will remain a priority, ensuring that future enhancements serve both employee well-being and client satisfaction. 
 As they grow, Wire2Air is committed to maintaining a high-trust, transparent culture that supports sustainable success in a competitive digital landscape.
