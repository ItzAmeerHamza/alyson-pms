 FAQ
 What is WebWork Time Tracker and how does it work? 
 WebWork Time Tracker is an ai-powered time tracking and employee monitoring software designed for remote, hybrid, and in-office teams. It helps you track working hours, monitor productivity, and manage tasks from a single dashboard. WebWork works by recording time spent on tasks, taking optional screenshots, analyzing app and website usage, and generating detailed reports so you can make data-driven decisions to boost performance.
 Are WebWork, WebWork Tracker, and WebWork Time Tracker the same? 
 Yes. WebWork Time Tracker is a software product created by WebWork AI, Inc. WebWork and WebWork Tracker are simply shorter names for WebWork Time Tracker.
 Who is WebWork for—can I use it for remote, hybrid, or in-office teams? 
 Yes. WebWork is built for remote teams, hybrid workplaces, freelancers, agencies, SMBs, and even large teams. Whether you're managing a small startup or scaling operations across departments, WebWork adapts to your team’s structure with powerful features for time tracking, attendance monitoring, project management, and real-time productivity tracking. 
 It’s the perfect solution for businesses that need to manage distributed workforces, optimize in-office workflows, or simply gain more control over how time is spent—no matter the size of your organization.
 What features does WebWork offer besides time tracking? 
 WebWork goes beyond simple time tracking. You also get:
 Screenshot monitoring (blurred, manual, or random modes)
 App and website usage tracking 
 Task and project management tools 
 Attendance and shift scheduling 
 Payroll and invoice generation 
 AI Assistant for daily activity report summaries 
 These features help improve team accountability, streamline workflows , and enhance productivity across your organization.
 Is WebWork secure and compliant with privacy regulations? 
 Absolutely. WebWork uses encrypted data transmission and is GDPR-compliant, ensuring your team’s data is protected and private. Employees can choose screenshot modes, and all tracking is transparent. You’re in full control of what’s monitored and when—building trust while maintaining accountability.
