 The Challenges Centaur Billing Faced
 Before implementing Time Champ, Centaur Billing struggled with:
 Lack of Insight into Breaks & Lost Time Managers had no clear visibility into how much time was lost during breaks and how effectively employees were using their time. 
 Manual & Inaccurate Attendance Tracking Attendance tracking was manual and error-prone, making it difficult to ensure accuracy, especially for remote employees. 
 Security Concerns & Compliance Risks No proper monitoring system to track suspicious activities, leading to potential compliance violations in a highly regulated industry. 
 The Solution Time Champ Offered
 To address these challenges, Centaur Billing implemented Time Champ, which provided a customized workforce management solution with key features like:
 Break Analysis for Improved Productivity Custom reports showing where time was lost and how break time was spent, allowing managers to optimize schedules without disrupting work-life balance. 
 Automatic Attendance for Accuracy & Transparency Automated clock-ins and clock-outs eliminated manual tracking errors, ensuring accurate attendance records. 
 Advanced Security & Monitoring Real-time monitoring through screenshots, screen recordings, and suspicious activity alerts helped detect and prevent unauthorized activities. 
 Time Champ improved Centaur Billing productivity, accuracy, security, and transparency, making workforce management easier.
