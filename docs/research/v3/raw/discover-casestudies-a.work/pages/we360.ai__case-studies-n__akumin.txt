 The Challenge Managing 1,200 Employees Across Five Plants Without Visibility 
 Akumin’s growing operations required a more efficient way to manage workforce activity, attendance, and productivity across its teams. Managers were spending valuable time gathering employee data, monitoring attendance, and compiling reports from multiple sources.
 Without a centralized workforce management system, it was difficult to get a clear, real-time view of employee activity and performance. This created additional administrative work for managers and made it harder for HR and leadership teams to access accurate workforce insights when they needed them.
 Manual reporting consumed 11+ hours/week per manager
 Every Monday was spent pulling data from five different systems and reconciling discrepancies.
 Zero real-time visibility into shift productivity
 Plant managers didn't know how a shift was performing until the next morning's report.
 Compliance gaps created regulatory risk
 Inconsistent time-tracking made labour law audits stressful and error-prone.
 High-performers going unrecognised
 Without data, star employees and underperformers looked identical on paper.

 Find quick answers to common questions about We360.ai
 What is We360.ai? We360.ai is an AI-powered workforce analytics platform for teams from 5 to 100,000+ employees. It gives operations and HR leaders real-time visibility into productivity, attendance, attrition risk, cost, and technology usage. Starting at ₹399 per user per month with custom enterprise pricing for larger rollouts.
 How much does We360.ai workforce analytics cost? We360.ai starts at ₹399 per user per month (approximately $4 USD). No credit card is required for the free 7-day trial. Plans scale with team size. For Enterprise quotes, see our pricing page.
 Can We360.ai replace employee monitoring software? Yes. We360.ai covers the core use cases of employee monitoring i.e. attendance, productivity trends, technology usage. While defaulting to non-invasive settings: no mandatory screen recording, no keystroke logging. Deeper features enable with employee consent notifications. 
 Does We360.ai work for hybrid and remote teams? Yes. We360.ai is built for teams with a mix of remote, in-office, and hybrid employees. Productivity and attendance tracked across all work locations in one dashboard. No separate setups for different location types.
 What is the difference between workforce management software and workforce analytics software? Workforce management software focuses on scheduling, shift planning, and labor compliance. Workforce analytics software like We360.ai goes deeper: analyzing productivity patterns, identifying bottlenecks, and surfacing insights about how work actually gets done. We360.ai combines both.
 How does We360.ai handle workforce privacy and compliance for India + global teams? We360.ai is consent-first by design. Employees see what's collected through their personal dashboard, and managers see analytics rolled up rather than raw monitoring feeds. Four certifications - SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, and HIPAA which covers the international standards Indian BPOs, healthcare, and finance buyers evaluate. The product collects only declared work-related behavioral data, supports in-app consent notifications, and does not sell or share workforce data with third parties. Audit reports, DPAs, and BAAs available on request.
 Which certifications does We360.ai hold? We360.ai holds SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, and HIPAA certifications, all independently verified. For enterprise security documentation, audit reports, DPAs, and BAAs, contact sales.
 What team sizes is We360.ai workforce analytics best suited for? We360.ai is built for teams of any size from 5-person ops teams to 100,000+ employee enterprise roll outs. Setup takes under 15 minutes regardless of scale, and pricing scales accordingly: ₹399/user/month at the mid-market end, custom enterprise pricing for larger deployments.
