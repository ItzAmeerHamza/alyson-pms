 The Challenges Vserve Ebusiness Solutions Faced 
 Vserve Ebusiness Solutions approached Time Champ with a clear need in mind: to regain 
 control over workforce 
 efficiency and improve operational visibility. 
 When asked about the challenges Vserve faced, Mr. Arun shared this, 
 “We’re a dynamic team handling critical eCommerce operations, but tracking productivity 
 has become increasingly 
 difficult. Employees often arrived late, and there was no proper way to capture or act 
 on this. We tried other 
 tools, but they were too expensive for us, lacked support, and didn’t give us the 
 visibility we needed.” 
 Other than this, they highlighted several specific challenges that were affecting their 
 ability to manage and 
 measure team performance effectively: 
 Low productivity across teams with no consistent way to measure improvement. 
 Employees clocking in late, without a system to monitor or flag such behaviors. 
 High operational costs due to expensive third-party tools with limited ROI. 
 Lack of integration with their HR portal, causing disconnects between systems. 
 Inadequate attendance monitoring causing workforce accountability difficult to enforce. 
 The Solution Time Champ Offered 
 To address these operational challenges, Vserve Ebusiness Solutions partnered with Time 
 Champ, a 
 robust workforce 
 management platform built to improve visibility, track productivity, and support real-time 
 decision-making. 
 Time Champ offered the following key solutions to enhance team accountability and refine 
 workforce operations: 
 Enabled real-time employee monitoring, giving managers full visibility into work hours, 
 application usage, and task activity, ensuring better control over daily workflows. 
 Tracked employee productivity metrics, helping supervisors identify performance 
 patterns, 
 reduce distractions, and motivate teams to stay focused on priority tasks. 
 Implemented precise time tracking, which directly helped reduce instances of late 
 arrivals 
 while promoting employee accountability. 
 Provided workplace analytics, offering insights into how teams operated, highlighting 
 workflow bottlenecks, and helping to refine task allocations and overall process 
 efficiency. 
 Integrated seamlessly with existing HR systems, enabling smooth syncing of attendance 
 data, employee profiles, and leave records. 
 By adopting Time Champ, Vserve Ebusiness Solutions gained greater operational clarity,

 workforce efficiency, and created a culture of transparency and accountability, without
