 FAQ
 What financial services leaders ask us most
 What is Shadow AI and how does Insightful detect it?
 Shadow AI is any AI tool someone uses without approval from IT or compliance, and sensitive data can go into it. Insightful surfaces every AI tool in use across your teams, how often each is open and which teams have it, so an unapproved tool is visible before it becomes exposure. What it shows is usage rather than intent: it will tell you a team opened a tool forty times last week, and not what they put into it.
 How does Insightful help financial services teams with compliance monitoring?
 It produces evidence. It does not discharge an obligation, and that distinction is the first thing a compliance officer checks. Insightful is a work insights platform. It collects hours, application use and meeting load from desk-based teams and reports them by team, each measure against the period before. Kept continuously, that becomes a record of how work moved through a back-office process, there before anyone asks and exportable when they do. Your program still owns the controls, the testing and the attestation.
 Does Insightful work for remote and hybrid financial services teams?
 Yes, and the reporting is the same wherever someone sits. It deploys on-premise or in a private cloud, supports teams across multiple locations, and infers working setting from the network rather than from GPS. The report says office or remote and nothing finer. It does not report an individual's physical location, which answers the next question.
 How does Insightful keep sensitive financial data secure?
 Certified: SOC 2 Type II. Compliant: HIPAA, GDPR and CCPA, which are regulations rather than certifications, and being exact about which is which is the point. Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2, with keys in Google Cloud KMS. On-premise or private cloud installation keeps it off shared infrastructure, and access is scoped by role. The control that matters most is what gets collected at all: applications and pages used and for how long, with no keystrokes, no audio or video and no content of any message or document.
 How quickly can financial services teams see results with Insightful?
 A first read arrives within 14 days. It runs on the desktop application with little change to how people already work. The first two weeks show which AI tools are in use, where utilization is lowest, and how time splits across the steps of whichever processes you scope. What two weeks cannot tell you is whether a process has drifted, because that needs a period to compare against.
