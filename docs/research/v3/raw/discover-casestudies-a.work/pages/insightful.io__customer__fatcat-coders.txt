 Losing Track When Working Remotely
 ‍
 Difficulty managing distributed teams.
 Lack of visibility into remote work.
 Lack of understanding of workload capacity & productivity.
 Unable to set realistic goals and measure productivity.
 Searching for a way to replace time-consuming constant check-ins.
 The Challenge: A Lack of Remote Work Transparency
 ‍
 Zoran Lazic, founder of FatCat Coders , a software development company, knew that work flexibility leads to more engaged, happy, and productive employees.
‍
 Lazic envisioned a flexible workplace where employees could work on their terms. An environment where tasks successfully completed was the key metric, not a rigid 8-hour work day.
‍
 But, like most workplaces, constant distractions hindered productivity at FatCat Coders. Making matters worse, when FatCat Coders transitioned to remote work, company leaders lost visibility into workloads and capacity.
‍
 Remote work became messy. Keeping remote employees on task required constant, time-consuming check-ins. This hampered business growth and prevented the flexible, engaged workplace Lazic envisioned.
 From Uncertainty to Clarity With the Help of Insightful
 ‍
 FatCat Coders’s managers tried traditional tracking tools without success. After a demo of Insightful, it was clear that Insightful would provide them with the full remote work visibility they’d been searching for. 
‍
 The platform also enabled FatCat Coders managers to see workload capacity and productivity trends at a glance from their Insightful dashboard, and dig deeper through comprehensive reports.
‍
 And it was in Insightful’s reports where FatCat Coders struck gold.
 ‍
 FatCat Coders Unearths a Data Goldmine
Insightful’s work activity and technology usage reporting revealed some FatCat Coders employees completed their work in just six hours daily. The best practices of these employees were identified, then managers used them to coach other employees to work more efficiently. 
‍
 This team-wide efficiency enables Lazic to offer all employees a flexible, six hour workday. Employees love this flexibility. They also choose when they work, and Insightful verifies work is getting done.
‍
 After four years using Insightful, FatCat Coders employees are more satisfied and productive, and the perk of flexibility helps attract talent.
 Insightful's been a game-changer for us. The insights we get are priceless for planning and keeping everything running smoothly.
 Zoran Lazic 
 CEO of FatCat Coders
 Flexibility is Great For Employees & Great for the Company
 ‍
 Insightful empowered FatCat Coders with the clarity and tools needed to finally implement their dream of a truly flexible workplace. This flexibility led to an immediate surge in job satisfaction and productivity. 
 ‍
 Unsure on how to introduce new productivity tools? For FatCat Coders communication and transparency with employees was the key to a fast, smooth adoption.
‍
 Implementing Insightful Also Led To…
 ‍
 Increased Employee Engagement
 Employees enjoy the flexibility of working when they want.
 More engaged employees improves retention and hiring.
 Proof of work gives managers confidence that work is being completed.
 Enhanced Operational Efficiency
 Managers are able to accurately analyze employee output.
 By comparing employee output, FatCat Coders identifies best practices.
 Employees are coached based on best practices to improve performance.
 Better Workload Management
 Clear visibility into task allocation helps FatCat Coders balance workloads.
 Managers can see how much time employees work on specific tasks.
 Free time is repurposed for education or put towards other projects in the company.
 More Effective Planning & Goal-Setting
 Managers have a true understanding of task duration and employee capacity.
 Setting goals and allocating resources is now done more accurately.
 Accurate capacity analysis helps avoid over utilization or underutilization.
 Minimizing Idle Time
 Insightful’s work activity data identifies and reduces unproductive periods.
 Idle time is corrected through coaching or by adjusting shifts to when employees are most productive.
 Eliminating idle time has increased the efficiency of tasks.
 Insightful has made our work lives so much easier. Our productivity and morale have never been better.
 Zoran Lazic 
 CEO of FatCat Coders
