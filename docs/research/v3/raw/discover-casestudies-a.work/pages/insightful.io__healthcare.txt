 FAQ
 What operations leaders ask us most
 What is healthcare workforce management software and how does Insightful use it? 
 Healthcare workforce management software reports how an organization is staffed and where its hours go. Insightful is a work insights platform. It collects hours, application use and meeting load from desk-based teams and reports them by team, each measure against the period before. In a healthcare back office that means timesheets that build themselves, workload you can see before a backlog forms, and a last-opened date against every paid license. It reads no patient record, and rostering the clinical floor is not what it does.
 How is Insightful different from clinical workforce management healthcare tools? 
 Clinical tools schedule the floor: shifts, credentials, acuity and coverage at the bedside. Insightful covers the back office instead, which is admin, billing, coding and IT, with the same visibility the clinical systems already have. It answers a different question for a different budget holder, and the two run side by side.
 What does healthcare workforce management look like for back-office and admin teams? 
 Four things, and they belong to four different people. HR stops chasing timesheets, because hours come from activity rather than from typing. Operations sees who is overloaded before a backlog forms. Finance gets a last-opened date against every paid seat before the renewal. IT gets per-team scope and an on-premise option. Reporting lands at team level by default rather than on a named individual, which is what keeps a rollout out of an employee-relations conversation.
 Does Insightful offer workforce management for healthcare organizations without a clinical focus? 
 Yes, and that is the whole scope. It is built for operations directors, COOs, IT managers and the finance lead running the business side of healthcare, not for clinical department heads. The line is the computer and the function rather than the specialty, so provider back offices, billing companies and medical suppliers are all covered. Clinical staff moving between rooms, and home-health and transport staff, are not: without a primary computer they do not appear in the data at all.
 How does Insightful keep sensitive healthcare data secure? 
 Certified: SOC 2 Type II. Compliant: HIPAA, GDPR and CCPA, which are regulations rather than certifications, and being exact about which is which is the point. Underneath that, AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2 in transit, keys in Google Cloud KMS, and residency in the US, the EU or Saudi Arabia, or on-premise, so patient health information (PHI) stays off shared infrastructure. Insightful records which applications and pages were used and for how long. It does not log keystrokes, record audio or video, or read the content of messages, email or documents. Screenshots are off unless an admin enables them, are blurred by default, and can be disabled entirely for teams working near patient records.
