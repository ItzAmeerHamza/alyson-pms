 We are giving up our commercial real estate and the associated expenses!
 Jim Garrettson
 Executive Mosaic 
 Manila, Philippines is unfortunately plagued with some of the world’s worst traffic. In-office workers were commuting an average of 2 hours each way. Executive Mosaic began searching for ways to allow a partial work from home solution in 2019 and began utilizing Time Doctor’s capabilities to ease the burden on its workforce. The transition and remote work test proved to be timely when COVID-19 hit us all.
 As the COVID-19 pandemic struck and created a “new normal,” Executive Mosaic was then able to implement Time Doctor for its entire workforce, including 88 team members in the Philippines just in time to send everyone home.
 “It looked like a stroke of unintentional genius,” Garrettson said. But he admits, “we were just lucky to be ahead of the curve.”
 Prior to using Time Doctor, Executive Mosaic had a traditional office
 based business structure that was extremely limiting in terms of what 
workers the company could attract and from what distance.
 For 
instance, Jim wanted to hire a product manager who would have been 
absolutely perfect for the job. However, the candidate took another job 
closer to home when she learned that she would have had to commute two 
hours each way to work.
 With Time Doctor, Jim has now made the 
decision to close his corporate offices in the Philippines which saves 
the company tens of thousands of dollars every year AND it opens 
Executive Mosaic’s reach up to hire talent from a local pool to a global
 pool . Executive Mosaic will also have access to shared commercial space
 options if space is needed for meetings.
 Executive Mosaic isn’t 
restricted to hire or recruit specifically from the available workforce 
in Metro Manila. The Philippines consists of over 7,000 islands and many 
remote provinces. The country is also now wired with internet access, so
 the company’s newly empowered virtual business has fresh options that 
were unthinkable before.
