 FAQ
 What enterprise leaders ask us most
 What is insurance workforce management software? 
 It reports how the office side of an insurance operation spends its hours. That covers claims and adjusting, underwriting support, policy service and the back office around them. Insightful is a work insights platform. It collects hours, application use and meeting load from desk-based teams and reports them by team, each measure against the period before. It covers carriers and third-party administrators, and the line is the computer: field adjusters working out of a vehicle are outside it.
 How is this different from our claims system's own reporting? 
 Your claims system reports what happened to the file: the stages, the handoffs, how long it took. What it cannot see is the work around the file, which is where the hours are. Insightful reports those hours and the applications behind each stage, which is how a slow claim gets traced to a system or a workload rather than left as a claims problem. It knows nothing about the claim itself, so the two are read side by side.
 Does this automate claims or route files? 
 No. It reports how the work happens; it does not act on it. Nothing here approves, denies, routes or settles anything, and no rule you write in Insightful will move a file through your claims system. What it does is show you which steps take the longest and which are the same every time, so you know where automation in your own system would pay off before you build it.
 Can this be used as evidence in a market conduct review? 
 It supplies evidence; it is not a compliance program and will not satisfy a review on its own. A market conduct review is a regulator's examination of how a carrier handles claims and treats policyholders. With the Audit Logs add-on, every admin change is held with who made it and when, in a form no user can alter. Documentation on the underlying controls is available under NDA, and it is worth asking for before a review rather than during one.
 Does this read the contents of claim files? 
 No. It records which applications and pages were used and for how long, and it logs no keystrokes, records no audio or video, and reads the content of no message, email or document. It does not see claim images, payment records or policyholder data of any kind. What it does see is how long an adjuster spent in the policy system against how long in the spreadsheet beside it. If that is more than a particular team should expose, collection scope is set per team before the desktop application is deployed.
