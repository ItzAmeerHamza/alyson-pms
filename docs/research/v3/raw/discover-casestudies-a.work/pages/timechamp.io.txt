 Frequently Asked Questions About Time Champ 
 What does Time Champ employee monitoring software track? 
 Time Champ employee monitoring software tracks real-time app and website usage, active and idle time, 
 automated screenshots at configurable intervals, keyboard and mouse activity intensity, attendance and shift 
 patterns, daily activity timelines, and suspicious behavior alerts. All data is visible in real-time 
 dashboards and downloadable reports. Works across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, and Chrome OS for 
 remote, hybrid, and in-office teams. 
 How does automatic time tracking software work? 
 Automatic time tracking software records work hours without any manual input from employees. As soon as an 
 employee starts working, whether opening a task or arriving at a job site, Time Champ begins logging time 
 and allocates hours to the correct projects and tasks automatically. Timesheets are generated without any 
 manual input, reviewed by managers, and approved with one click, eliminating timesheet errors, billing 
 disputes, and manual HR reconciliation every pay cycle. 
 What does employee productivity tracking software measure? 
 Employee productivity tracking software measures total productive hours per employee, app and website usage 
 classified as productive, neutral, or unproductive, focus time and context-switch frequency, idle time and 
 distraction patterns, and productivity scores compared to personal and team benchmarks. Time Champ also 
 generates visual heatmaps showing when each employee is most focused, helping managers schedule 
 high-priority work during peak productivity windows. 
 Can Time Champ manage projects and track time against specific tasks? 
 Yes. Time Champ includes full project management software with Kanban boards, task assignment, deadline 
 tracking, 
 and milestone management. Time is automatically tracked against every task as employees work, creating a 
 real-time 
 link between project progress and actual hours spent. Managers get project time reports that connect 
 directly to 
 client billing and payroll, with capacity utilization views showing who has bandwidth for new assignments. 
 Does Time Champ support field staff management and remote employee monitoring? 
 Yes. Time Champ field staff management software provides live GPS location tracking, geo-fenced attendance, 
 mobile 
 time logging with offline sync, and route monitoring for field employees. Remote employee monitoring works 
 through a 
 lightweight background agent that tracks activity, logs hours, and captures screenshots without disrupting 
 workflows. Both field and remote data feed into the same central dashboard, giving managers unified 
 visibility 
 across all work locations. 
 What makes Time Champ different from basic employee monitoring software? 
 Basic employee monitoring software stops at activity logs, it shows who clocked in and what apps they 
 used. Time Champ adds a workforce intelligence layer that turns those signals into focus patterns, 
 productivity scores, capacity trends, and burnout risks. The result is data managers and finance teams 
 can act on, not just review. 
 Is Time Champ compliant with GDPR, HIPAA, and ISO 27001? 
 Yes. Time Champ is GDPR-compliant, ISO 27001:2022 certified, HIPAA-compliant, and SOC 2 Type I certified. 
 Employee monitoring is transparent: employees are informed that monitoring is active, and all data 
 collection is proportionate to operational needs. Organizations control what is monitored, how screenshots 
 are stored, and who has access. Complete audit trails and role-based access controls support regulatory 
 review at any time.
