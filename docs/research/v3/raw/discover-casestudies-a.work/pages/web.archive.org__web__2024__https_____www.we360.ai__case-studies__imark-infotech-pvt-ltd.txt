 Driving Productivity and Empowering Growth: iMark Infotech's Success with We360.ai
 Company
 iMark Infotech Pvt Ltd
 Company size
 200-500
 Industry
 IT Services & Consulting
 Location
 Chandigarh, India
 iMark Infotech Pvt Ltd, a prominent IT services and IT consulting company, faced the challenge of optimising their operations, enhancing employee productivity, and gaining deeper insights into their workforce. In their quest for a comprehensive solution, they turned to We360.ai. This detailed case study explores how We360.ai's advanced workforce analytics and monitoring capabilities empowered iMark Infotech to overcome their challenges and achieve remarkable results.
 Through We360.ai, we sparked innovation and reshaped our operations, maximizing resources and igniting growth.
 Challenge 
 iMark Infotech identified several critical challenges that hindered their efficiency and growth:
 Lack of Visibility: 
 iMark Infotech struggled with limited visibility into their employees' activities, making it difficult to track performance, identify top performers, and optimize resource allocation. They needed a solution to gain real-time insights into employee productivity.
 ‍
 Internet Activity: 
 With a diverse range of services and departments, iMark Infotech found it time-consuming and cumbersome to identify new applications and tools relevant to each team's specific requirements. They sought a streamlined approach to discover and implement innovative resources.
 ‍
 Solution 
 We360.ai provided iMark Infotech with a comprehensive solution tailored to their unique challenges, offering a wide array of powerful features and functionalities:
 ‍
 Activity Monitoring 
 We360.ai's robust activity monitoring feature goes beyond traditional time tracking. By capturing various activity levels such as mouse movements, key presses, and mouse clicks, it provides a comprehensive understanding of employee engagement. For iMark Infotech, this meant gaining valuable insights into their workforce's time distribution, including online time, offline time, working time, idle time, active time, and inactive time.
 ‍
 With We360.ai's activity monitoring, iMark Infotech could delve deeper into employee behavior patterns and productivity trends. They could identify when employees were most engaged, highlight potential areas of distraction or inefficiency, and take data-driven actions to optimize their performance. By having a clear view of their employees' time allocation, iMark Infotech could implement targeted strategies to boost productivity and maximize operational efficiency.
 ‍
 Application Tracking 
 We360.ai's advanced analytics in application tracking empowered iMark Infotech to understand how their employees utilized different applications. They gained insights into app productivity, identifying which applications were productive, unproductive, or neutral in contributing to their business objectives. This helped iMark Infotech evaluate and streamline their application stack, ensuring that employees had access to the most effective and relevant tools for their tasks.
 Moreover, We360.ai provided iMark Infotech with a comprehensive overview of the entire list of applications and URLs utilized by their employees. This appwise utilization data allowed iMark Infotech's administrators to assess the software landscape within their organization and make informed decisions about application usage and licensing. With teamwise utilization insights, iMark Infotech could identify trends and preferences among different departments, facilitating cross-functional collaboration and resource optimization.
 ‍
 By leveraging We360.ai's activity monitoring and application tracking features, iMark Infotech gained granular visibility into their workforce's activities and application usage. This enabled them to identify areas for improvement, streamline workflows, and foster a culture of productivity and innovation across the organization. With the power of these features, iMark Infotech was able to maximize their resources, enhance employee engagement, and drive overall business success.

 Results 
 The implementation of We360.ai yielded remarkable results for iMark Infotech, revolutionising their workforce management and driving significant improvements across various aspects of their operations. Here are some key outcomes they achieved:
 ‍
 Enhanced Productivity and Performance: 
 With We360.ai's activity monitoring and application tracking capabilities, iMark Infotech gained real-time insights into their employees' work patterns and productivity levels. This visibility enabled them to identify top performers, track performance trends, and optimize resource allocation for maximum efficiency.
 ‍
 Streamlined Resource Allocation: 
 By leveraging We360.ai's detailed reports on employee activity and application usage, iMark Infotech could make data-driven decisions regarding resource allocation. They could identify the most productive applications and allocate resources accordingly, streamlining their operations and ensuring optimal utilization of resources.
 ‍
 Cultivating a Culture of Self-Evaluation: 
 iMark Infotech recognized the importance of self-evaluation and continuous improvement for their employees. They utilized the reports generated by We360.ai to share insights on individual performance and work trends with all employees internally. This practice empowered employees to evaluate their own work, identify areas for growth, and make targeted improvements, fostering a culture of self-improvement and professional development.
 ‍
 Increased Efficiency in Workflows: 
 With the ability to analyze employees' engagement levels and time utilization, iMark Infotech optimized their workflows and eliminated potential areas of inefficiency. By understanding how employees engaged with their laptops and managed their time, they could identify work patterns and implement strategies to enhance productivity and streamline processes.
 ‍
 Fostered Collaboration and Innovation: 
 We360.ai's application tracking feature enabled iMark Infotech to identify applications and URLs used by employees, facilitating collaboration and knowledge-sharing across different teams. By understanding the software preferences and utilization patterns, iMark Infotech fostered an environment of collaboration, innovation, and cross-functional learning.

 Key Takeaways 
 iMark Infotech's success story with We360.ai offers valuable insights for businesses seeking to optimize their workforce and drive growth:
 ‍
 Embrace Activity Analytics: 
 Businesses can gain a comprehensive understanding of employee productivity by leveraging advanced activity monitoring. Real-time insights enable organizations to identify areas for improvement, optimize processes, and drive overall performance.

 Foster Collaboration and Innovation: 
 Tracking application usage and encouraging exploration of new tools unlocks innovation potential. By facilitating cross-departmental collaboration and empowering employees to embrace new technologies, organizations can stay ahead of the competition and drive continuous improvement.

 Optimize Resource Allocation: 
 Identifying and leveraging preferred applications and tools of top performers enables organizations to streamline resource allocation. By ensuring that teams have access to the right resources, businesses can enhance workflow efficiency, maximize productivity, and make informed decisions about resource allocation.
