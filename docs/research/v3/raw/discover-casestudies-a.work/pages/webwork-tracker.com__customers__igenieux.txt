 The Problems IGENIEUX Faced Before WebWork
 We wanted to make sure our people weren’t just clocking in—but actually working. We needed a tool that would connect timekeeping with real productivity.
 Karen Mae Beniabon, HR Officer at IGENIEUX 
 Before adopting WebWork, IGENIEUX struggled with fragmented processes across time tracking, payroll, and productivity management. As the company began to scale both its remote and on-site teams, their existing tools, and manual processes fell short, causing delays, inaccuracies, and frustration across departments
 Payroll inaccuracies and compliance challenges
 With the complexity of Philippine labor laws and government-mandated deductions, accurate payroll was a critical need. But without a reliable time tracker, the team at IGENIEUX were forced to manually verify work hours. This would often mean relying on screenshots or employee claims, leading to potential errors and inconsistent records.
 Complications with Toggl and Hubstaff
 IGENIEUX initially tried Toggl and Hubstaff, but both tools proved problematic. Toggl lacked the necessary payroll integrations and screenshots, while Hubstaff, although functional, was too complex and error-prone for everyday use. The team often found themselves dealing with bugs, overly technical dashboards, or unclear data exports.
 Manual attendance tracking
 In locations like Siargao, where biometric devices weren’t available, tracking attendance became a logistical issue. Managers couldn’t easily confirm punctuality or productivity, and employees lacked a transparent system to validate their working hours.
 No visibility into productivity
 With no way to measure how employees spent their time once clocked in, there was no safeguard against passive time or misuse of work hours. Managers had no clear view of employee output but only clock-in and clock-out times from other platforms.
 How WebWork Solved the Tracking and Visibility Issues
 After struggling with faulty and inaccurate yet pricey time trackers like Toggl and Hubstaff, INAGENIUX needed a tool that would track work hours accurately, provide proof of work, and not cost a fortune. When the team turned to WebWork they saw the difference immediately. It was the reliable and scalable solution that checks all the boxes.
 WebWork not only replaced multiple tools but also connected time tracking with productivity, attendance, and payroll. It allowed HR to gain clarity, managers to make faster decisions, and employees to clearly understand expectations.
 The HR team now spends less time verifying work logs and more time focusing on strategy. Onboarding is faster, payroll is more accurate, and both remote and on-site teams are held to the same transparent standards. With every feature working together in a single system, WebWork gave IGENIEUX the structure it needed to grow efficiently and confidently.
