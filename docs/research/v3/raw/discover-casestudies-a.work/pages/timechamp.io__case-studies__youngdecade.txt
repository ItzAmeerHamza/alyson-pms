 The Challenges Young Decade Faced
 Young Decade faced significant difficulties in managing their growing team and 
 multiple projects. Their existing approach lacked visibility, making it hard to track productivity, 
 allocate resources, and meet deadlines. These are the main challenges they faced:
 Difficulty in Tracking Work Hours – Struggled to monitor how much 
 time employees spent on tasks and projects. 
 Lack of Centralized Project Management – No single platform to assign 
 tasks, track progress, or ensure deadlines were met. 
 Complicated HR Processes – Managing attendance, leave tracking, and 
 performance evaluations was inefficient and time-consuming. 
 Use of Multiple Disconnected Tools – Relying on separate tools for 
 different needs led to inefficiencies and a lack of overall visibility. 
 Limited Productivity Insights – No easy way to analyze employee 
 performance or identify areas for improvement.

 Enhanced Time Tracking & Productivity Monitoring – Tracks employee work 
 hours and activities accurately, improving accountability. Identifies unproductive time to optimize 
 efficiency.
