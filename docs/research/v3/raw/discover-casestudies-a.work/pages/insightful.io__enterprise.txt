 FAQ
 What enterprise leaders ask us most
 What is workforce analytics software and how does Insightful use it? 
 Workforce analytics software turns data about how work happens into decisions about capacity, cost and process. Insightful is a work insights platform. It collects hours, application use and meeting load from desk-based teams and reports them by team, each measure against the period before. At enterprise scale it combines task mining, workforce analytics and work intelligence in one, from a single desktop application deployed through your MDM. It does not tell you why a number moved. It tells you which team and which week to go and ask about.
  How does Insightful help executive teams improve EBITDA? 
 Two of the lines are countable without a model: hours recovered and licenses retired. Output is reported by team and by site, and every tool is ranked by real use with a last-opened date against each paid seat, so a renewal is resized before it is signed. A process fix converts to cost at your own rate. There is no single blended ROI number, and an enterprise finance team would discount one anyway.
 How is Insightful different from other workforce analytics solutions? 
 Three differences are worth checking against whatever else is on your shortlist. The span: hours, productivity and focus, application and license use, and work setting all come off one record and one desktop application, where elsewhere that takes more than one vendor and a reconciliation step. The posture: narrow by design, the shape of work rather than its content, with every control set before deployment. And the default: reporting lands at team level, not on a named individual.
 What does a workforce analytics platform give executives that dashboards don't? 
 A dashboard reports the outputs your systems of record already hold. This reports the work behind them, measured as work happens rather than reconstructed afterward: where the hours went by team and by site, which applications and AI tools the quarter ran on, and which paid seats nobody opened. That is the difference between a quarter that reconciles and a named team with a named process attached to the gap.
 What should executives look for in workforce analytics tools? 
 Start with what the desktop application refuses to capture. That is the question a legal reviewer opens with, and the one that stalls a rollout late. Then three more: whether reporting lands at team level or names an individual by default, whether the read is continuous or a periodic audit you commission, and what the deployment needs. On-premise and private cloud are both available, and data residency covers the US, the EU and Saudi Arabia.
